Rules & Regulations

1.) Alliance Rules

  • Speak with respect to all members
  • Donate to alliance tech daily
  • No farming on TDTE, RSN1, MENA
  • Avoid world chat drama at all costs

2.) 357 United Rules and Regulations

  1. NAP 0 (Monday-Thursday), NAP All (Friday-Sunday) except those on punishment list.
  2. Drama Free World Chat (If there are issues then leaders need to talk to leaders and not have members bring the drama to world chat)
  3. Chain of Communication: Members should be talking to r4s, r4s to r5, and r5 to other r5s. If that doesn't fix anything then r5 to the presidential team.
  4. Grace Period Rules will have a 24-hour grace period so everyone can read it, Blacklisted start immediately (Punishment list and blacklist are 2 different lists)
  5. Corrective Action to be handed out by the server, not just the whales (If you don't want to hand out the punishment then don't complain about no punishments)
  6. SVS, we will be watching and addressing SVS participation very carefully (Alliance or people refusing to participate will be free to be farmed for an entire week)

BLACKLIST

  • Rule breaking will result in a removal from your alliance on Sunday and free to be farmed.
  • Punishments shall begin on Sundays from 12:00(noon) to 20:00 server time, allowing affected players to support their alliance (ex.: daily yeti) while still facing consequences.
  • If an R5 refuses to hand out punishments they will assume the punishment of their player.
  • Attempting to evade punishment (ex.: name changes, shielding, teleporting, etc.) will result in an extension: 3 days for the first attempt and 7 days for repeats
  • R5s on the punishment list may not receive reinforcements. Anyone providing reinforcement to a R5 will be added to the list as well.
  • The number of days on the list is cumulative based on the number of rules broken.

2.1) Farming Rules

  1. Maximum 3 attackers HQs per hive, reinforcements Included. (Ex.: If TDTE is farming WLF1 with 3 players, no one else from any other alliance can farm WLF1 until there are less than 3 players. There is a time limit of 1 hour per day in a hive)
  2. Tanks per HQ: If you are farming the same HQ with other players, you are allowed to use 2 tanks per player; if you are not farming the same HQ as others you are allowed up to 4 tanks.
  3. 2 Attacks per HQ: Only 2 sets of attack victories are allowed per HQ
  4. No Zeroing: There is still no zeroing/resetting of HQS that belong to an alliance.
  5. 4.1 Defend the hive: You are allowed to zero anyone who comes to your hive to farm, but once they leave you cannot chase them around the map to zero them. IF YOU ARE IN THE TOP 20 OF HERO POWER RANKING YOU CANNOT HELP DEFEND YOUR HIVE UNLESS A TOP 20 ATTACKS YOU
  6. 4.2 Attackers: If you are farming a hive and they start to attack you; you still cannot zero them as you are the aggressor in their hive.
  7. You cannot attack the same hive two days in a row.
  8. Non-Alliance HQs not in an alliance are free to all to be farmed and zeroed
  9. You cannot leave an alliance to attack someone and then re-join your alliance
  10. Truck: no stealing from 357 players
  11. City capture/Artic ice pit : Tvt only, no attack on HQs are permitted, others alliances are welcome to assist in defense and attack.
